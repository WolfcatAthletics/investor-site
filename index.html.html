The Wolfcat Lending Model

A Proven Model. A Faster Timeline.

We combine the mission-driven approach of NACA with the speed and structure of FHA and Freddie Mac underwriting frameworks.

Proven Mission, Proven Speed

NACA has demonstrated for over 30 years that mission-driven lending can work at scale. Their "Achieve the Dream" events prove the entire qualification process can happen in one day. We apply that same philosophy — but pair it with FHA/Freddie Mac's automated underwriting systems for a normal, predictable timeline.

FHA and Freddie Mac Frameworks

We underwrite using FHA 203(b) and Freddie Mac Home Possible methodology. These are federally backed, well-established programs with clear standards. Home Possible specifically allows qualified buyers to get a loan without a credit score. This is our pathway for low/no-score borrowers — not an exception, but a documented, regulatory-compliant path.

Education as Risk Mitigation

Every borrower completes HUD-certified first-time homebuyer education and financial literacy counseling. This is a proven risk mitigant. The Housing Financial Literacy Act would even discount FHA mortgage insurance premiums for first-time buyers who complete counseling. For our investors, this means lower delinquency and a responsible borrower base.

The FHA Counseling Fund Advantage

We leverage HUD-approved housing counseling funds where eligible. For pre-purchase counseling, we fund this ourselves as a core risk management cost — because a prepared borrower is a performing loan.

The Wolfcat Wealth & Resilience Fund

The Borrower's Hedge Fund

A TSP-style restricted annuity that replaces PMI — building a self-directed emergency fund in the borrower's name, managed by the CDFI as fiduciary, and cash-outable at exit.

Traditional PMI Wolfcat Wealth & Resilience Fund
Borrower pays 0.3–1.5% of loan amount annually Borrower pays 1% of asset value monthly
Protects the lender if borrower defaults Protects the borrower if financial hardship occurs
Money goes to a private insurance company Money goes into an annuity-like instrument with additional insurance
Borrower never sees the money again Funds are held in the borrower's name and can be used to assist them
No benefit to borrower during hardship CDFI (by proxy or POA) can use funds to assist the borrower during hardship

TSP Model

Borrowers contribute monthly to a restricted account managed by the CDFI as fiduciary. They can view their balance, request loans against it, and draw funds for hardship — all through a customer portal.

POA / Proxy Protection

For seniors and foster youth without familial support, the CDFI holds Power of Attorney or acts as Proxy. The only beneficiary to these funds is the homeowner. If they marry or gain family support, they can change the mechanism.

Track A vs. Track B

Track A: CDFI neighborhoods — no heavy HOA restrictions. The borrower pays the life of the loan.
Track B: Avoid clashing with existing HOAs for acquired properties. The borrower pays the life of the loan.

Exit & Family Assumption

At refinance or sale, the borrower cashes out the accumulated equity. If a family member wants to assume the loan, they must refinance at the current market price — unless the home is left in a will or trust.

"We are not inventing a lending model. We are adopting the best of what already works — NACA's mission, FHA's standards, Freddie Mac's speed — and deploying it through the Wolfcat CDFI."
